Transect

Transect is a free first-person walk through a northern valley that no one built by hand. Every birch, pine, fern and boulder in it comes from an open library of plant and stone generators, each one measured from a photograph, so the valley is different at every seed and true to the places it was drawn from. The game is a night out alone: you carry a pack, gather deadwood, lay and light a fire, pitch a tent before the cold arrives, and write the night into a journal. The sky is computed rather than painted — the Sun and Moon where they would be on that day, storms with held lightning, and on some nights the northern lights — and every sound is a CC0 field recording. It runs in a browser, on a phone or a desktop, and costs nothing. Transect is made by a one-person studio, and is the free prologue to Norge: Tales of the North, a game about keeping a cold coast alive.

Before you go

  • Give it a moment to load: the valley grows on your device while you read the start card.
  • Sound on if you can: the wind, the stream and the fire are most of it.
  • Try to see the dawn. The cold is the thing to beat.
  • Tell me what you think with the Feedback button on the start card, or what went wrong, the moment it does.
  • On a phone, Add to Home Screen opens it full screen next time.
